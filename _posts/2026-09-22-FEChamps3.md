# Fallen Empires World Champs III

Time flies! It’s that time of the year again – World Champs season! On 9/21/26 we hosted the third Fallen Empires 40 World Championships at Shuffler’s Den (the Elven Fortress of NE Ohio)! Folks from all around came to sling some of the most niche cardboard around (sleeves optional), in what has quickly risen to be one of my favorite events of the year! As usual, the event was heavy on door prizes and giveaways, as well as talent and respect for some of the deeper cuts of early MtG.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/1.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

If unfamiliar with <a href="https://clevelandrocs.net/formats/">Fallen Empires 40</a>, the format is a unique “rubber band” OS-adjacent format that uses Fallen Empires as the core set. “Traditional” FE40 gained some international popularity via <a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/468172425315016">webcam leagues</a> beginning in 2022, and has established itself as a beloved constructed format in Northeast Ohio and beyond.
15 mages set out to see who could control the fall of empires…

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/2.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

What would FE champs be without its swag? – matters were taken into format moderator Craig’s own hands to provide the slickest player lanyards I have ever seen for an event (I’ve been to a lot).

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/3.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/4.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

We were also lucky enough to have Mitch Harmon back as our “feature artist” and the talent behind the beloved FE promo lands seen now throughout many veins of the OS community! (If interested in these lands, you can <a href="mailto:mittys.palette@gmail.com">reach out directly</a>)

## The Tournament

Unlike last year’s event, which was rather Thallid/control heavy, rounds went quickly, with only a few games going to time, and only 1 drawn game! A diverse meta with some truly interesting deckbuilds highlighted what was a chess-board state, for 4 rounds of swiss. Fallen Empires can get a bad rep for various reasons, but this set design never was explored or flourished as I saw it this day. Truly, as I think Garfield would have intended it.

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/7.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

After 3 rounds, Akron legionnaires Adam and Matt faced off for the finals. After a very hard fought and interesting match (and a massive spore cloud), for the second year running, Matt Shields took the Trophy! This time with B/G Thallids! <a href="https://52weekbeta.blogspot.com/2026/09/fe-worlds-2026-gloves-off-mushrooms-on.html">His thoughts here</a>.

## The 2026 FE Champs list:

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/10.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

### Matt took full advantage of the “aggro” side of green.

Rather than the more often seen “spore flower control” build, he put the pedal to the metal and pushed every turn featuring Thallid Devourer and Derelor (a worthy splash in place of what could have been removal). Ferral Thallid and no Fungal Bloom were spicy as heck, and he cruised to a clean 4-0 record.

### Adam took second place with 8-order,

{% include figure.liquid loading="eager" path="assets/img/FEChamps3/11.jpg" class="img-fluid rounded z-depth-1" zoomable=true %}

This deck was sharp and resilient. Adam was a force to be reckoned with, including spirit shield to protect orders (often vulnerable to FE removal) and some excellent game play! Congrats Adam for making the finals with another unique build!

Top 4 included Craig Snook and Herb Wilson, both format veterans. Herb was in the finals in the very first online league, and succumbed to Matt last year in the ’25 championship game. He may be the winningest FE player without a trophy (and will be out for blood)… And we all know Craig – We wouldn’t be here without him <3
