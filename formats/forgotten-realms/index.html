Forgotten Realms is an immersive old-school wedge variant that focuses on late ’94–’95 Magic.

It uses the following ruleset:

  • Modern rules + mana burn
  • 60-card minimum main deck
  • 15-card maximum sideboard
  • Refer to Gatherer for current card text and card rulings

Legal sets

  • The Dark
  • Fallen Empires
  • Homelands

Reprint policy

Same art/same frame reprints are allowed.

Banned and restricted lists

Banned

  • An-Zerrin Ruins
  • Apocalypse Chime
  • Timmerian Fiends

Restricted to 1 copy

  • Hymn to Tourach
  • Maze of Ith
  • Serrated Arrows

Forgotten Realms is a sister format to FE40 and also features a semi-monthly global online league. The format is easy to enter, friendly to casual play, and well suited to kitchen-table or bar-room games.

For community discussion and league updates, join the Discord: Cleveland Old School Discord.
